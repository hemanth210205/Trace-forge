# Returns and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Eligibility
Customers are eligible to return purchased physical products if the item is in new, unused condition with all original tags, manuals, and accessories intact. Proof of purchase (order confirmation number or original receipt) is required for all returns.

## 2. Return Period
Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Return requests initiated after 30 calendar days from the confirmed delivery date cannot be accepted.

## 3. Refund Conditions
Approved returns will be refunded to the original payment method used during checkout. Items that show signs of wear, damage, or missing components may be subject to a 15% restocking fee or denied completely upon physical inspection.

## 4. Original Packaging Requirements
All returned products must be shipped in their original manufacturer packaging, including any protective padding, seals, and barcodes. If original packaging is lost, a replacement packaging fee of $10 will be deducted from the final refund.

## 5. Non-Returnable Products
The following items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable:
- Customized or personalized products
- Digital downloads, software licenses, and gift cards
- Clearance, final-sale, and promotional giveaway merchandise
- Opened intimate apparel and sanitary goods

## 6. Refund Processing Timeline
Once our warehouse receives the returned package, inspection is completed within 2 business days. The refund is processed to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after inspection approval. Depending on your financial institution, funds may take an additional 2 to 3 days to appear on your bank statement.
