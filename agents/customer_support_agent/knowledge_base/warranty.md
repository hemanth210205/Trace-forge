# Standard Hardware Warranty Policy

## 1. Warranty Duration
All hardware devices and electronic products purchased directly through our store come with a 1-year limited hardware warranty starting from the original purchase date. Extended warranty plans add 12 or 24 additional months if registered within 30 days of purchase.

## 2. Covered Defects
This warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and internal workmanship encountered during normal, residential use. Covered issues include motherboard component failure, defective charging ports, display panel hardware dead pixels, and unprovoked power failures.

## 3. Exclusions
This limited warranty strictly does not cover:
- Accidental damage, including drops, impacts, cosmetic scratches, and cracked exterior screens
- Liquid immersion, spills, moisture exposure, or flood/fire damage
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs, opening the chassis, or aftermarket modifications
- Normal wear and tear, cosmetic degradation, and natural battery capacity decline over time
- Theft, loss, or damage resulting from electrical power surges or third-party power adapters

## 4. Warranty Claims
To submit a warranty claim:
1. Locate your product serial number on the bottom label or invoice.
2. Prepare photo or video documentation clearly showing the defect.
3. Submit a ticket online with your order number and claim description.
Our technical triage team evaluates claim submissions within 48 hours.

## 5. Replacement Procedure
If your warranty claim is approved, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label for the defective unit. Upon receipt and verification at our repair depot, an equivalent new or certified factory-refurbished replacement unit will be dispatched within 3 business days at zero shipping cost to the customer.
