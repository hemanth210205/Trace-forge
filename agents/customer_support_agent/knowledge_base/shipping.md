# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Standard Delivery
Standard domestic delivery takes 3 to 5 business days from the dispatch date. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days are dispatched on the same day; orders placed on weekends or holidays ship on the next business day.

## 2. Express Delivery
Express domestic shipping delivers within 1 to 2 business days. Express orders placed before 3:00 PM EST qualify for guaranteed expedited transit via priority carrier couriers.

## 3. Delivery Estimates
Delivery estimates are calculated from the moment the package leaves our fulfillment center, not from the time of checkout. Estimated delivery dates for all active shipments can be verified using the order status tracking lookup.

## 4. Shipping Charges
- Standard Delivery: Free on all domestic orders of $50 or more. For orders under $50, a flat shipping fee of $4.99 applies.
- Express Delivery: Flat rate fee of $14.99 for all order sizes.
- International Shipping: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination country duties.

## 5. Delayed Shipment Policy
If your order has not arrived within 48 hours past the estimated delivery date, our logistics team automatically flags the carrier for investigation. If a shipment is delayed by more than 7 business days past the original estimate, the customer is eligible for a full refund or an immediate complimentary replacement reshipment.

## 6. Tracking Information
A shipping confirmation email with an active carrier tracking link and tracking number is dispatched as soon as the shipping label is generated. Tracking updates usually reflect within 12 hours of courier pickup.
